What not to demo
Do not walk through the Unity Gateway setup work. Registering endpoints, writing service policies, configuring budgets, and the agent investigation from Build 3 are build activities, not presentation material. The personas in the room do not care how the gateway was configured. They care that spending is bounded, visible, and attributable. Show the dashboard and its numbers, and skip the administration behind it.
